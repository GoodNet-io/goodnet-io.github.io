The Name

GoodNet-io — June 2026

There is a word older than any language.

Proto-Indo-Europeans called it ghedh- — and it meant one thing: what comes together. What finds its own. What fits, not by force, but by nature.

Millennia later, Germanic tribes said gōdaz. Fitting. Suitable. That which belongs where it is.

One root. Thousands of years. The same meaning.

And there is another word, older still.

Ned- — to bind, to tie. From it came nodus: the knot. From the knot came the mesh. From the mesh came the net.

Old English speakers knew what a net was for. They used the same word for a spider's web. And for a snare.

When the name came together — no one thought of roots. It simply felt right. Fitting. Gōdaz.

Then someone opened a dictionary.

And smiled.
